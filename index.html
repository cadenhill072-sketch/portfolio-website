No. 1

Machined Bracket Should-Cost Model

An Excel model of what a machined aluminum bracket should cost, built from LME aluminum, the Midwest premium, BLS wages and a machine rate I built from scratch. It lands at about $34.20 delivered against a made-up $38.90 quote, so the quote is about 12% high.

A flat 9% on the whole part because aluminum went up about 18%. That takes the price from $38.90 to $42.40.How the increase usually shows up.

The model also writes the price as a contract formula, so the metal moves with the market and the conversion price stays fixed.

No. 2

Industrial Supplier Risk Scorecard

One view of where supply risk sits across 30 made-up suppliers for a manufacturer that builds motion control equipment, from castings and machined parts to semiconductors and motors. Each supplier is scored on delivery, quality, finances, concentration, geography and contract timing.

As of October 2026, 4 of the 30 are high risk and hold about $29.6M, or 17%, of the $174.7M in spend. I built the scoring in Excel, as DAX measures for Power BI, and in JavaScript for the web version.
