Industrial Supplier Risk Scorecard

I scored 30 direct material suppliers for a made-up industrial manufacturer on six kinds of risk and plotted them against spend, to show which ones a sourcing team should deal with first.

Made with synthetic data. Every supplier, number and event on this page is invented, any match to a real company name is a coincidence, and the data was generated in Python.

Spend vs. risk

Each dot is a supplier. The dashed lines are the cutoffs that split the four actions. Click a dot to open that supplier.

Last 12 months, with each category's spend-weighted risk score.

Who to look at first

    Scorecard

    Each area is scored 0 (no risk) to 100, and darker cells mean more risk. Click a column header to sort or a row to open the supplier.

    How the score works

    Each area gets scaled from 0 to 100 using fixed cutoffs and then weighted. Delivery and quality use the last six months, and spend, category share and disruption events use the last twelve. Changing the as-of month rescores the whole page, so you can watch a supplier get worse over time.

    High risk is 50 and up, medium is 30 to 50, and low is under 30. The action split is 35 points of risk and 5 million dollars of spend.

    The same scoring is built three ways: Excel formulas in the workbook, DAX measures written for Power BI, and JavaScript for this page.
