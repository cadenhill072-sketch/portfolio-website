How I build these projects

The first three projects on my homepage started with curiosity about how generative AI tools could help with real sourcing decisions, like whether a price is fair, which supplier to worry about first, and how much stock to hold. They're independent projects I did on my own time.

For each one I picked the question, researched the inputs and decided what the model should help someone decide. I used AI tools as a partner to build it, then checked every number myself. I'm happy to walk through any of them.

The packaging spend tool and the carrier bid repository come from my internships at General Mills and TA Dedicated. Their pages are rebuilt with invented data, so nothing that belongs to either company is shown.
